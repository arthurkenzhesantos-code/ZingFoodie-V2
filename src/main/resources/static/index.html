How it works

  1. Choose available food and add it to your cart.
  2. Enter your name, campus role, campus, building, and classroom/section or office.
  3. Select classroom delivery or pickup and choose a preferred time.
  4. Confirm the order and keep your order number / QR receipt.
  5. Track status updates. Staff verifies the QR receipt at handoff.

Delivery rules

  • Orders are delivered only within the selected campus.
  • Classroom/office and section details are required for delivery.
  • Estimated time is an estimate and can change during peak hours.
  • Cancellation is available while an order is still pending.
  • Never share passwords or sensitive personal information in delivery notes.

About this prototype

Orders, accounts, menu stock and reports are managed by the Java backend and SQLite database. Staff access is restricted to staff accounts. QR receipts are generated by the server and verified once at handoff. Use only approved campus buildings and protect your login details.
