Help & campus policies

Simple rules for smooth campus ordering.

How it works

  1. Choose food and add it to your cart.
  2. Enter your campus and delivery information.
  3. Confirm your order and keep its order number.
  4. Staff will update your order status.

Delivery rules

  • Delivery is only within the campus selected.
  • Estimated delivery time can change during busy hours.
  • Only pending orders may be cancelled.
  • Never include passwords or sensitive information in delivery notes.

Account and data

ZingFoodie uses Firebase Authentication for sign-in and Firestore for account profiles, menu data, and orders. Your orders are associated with your account. Use the same email to access your account on another device.
